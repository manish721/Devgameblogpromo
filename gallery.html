Event Unacademy Fest

The Presentation

Business begins with being human.

At the Unacademy Fest, I had the opportunity to present DevGameBlogPromo and share a perspective that goes beyond products, services and business strategies.

The central idea of the presentation was simple: before building an organization, a business or a career, we must first understand the importance of being human.

I spoke about how the way we think, the values we follow and the decisions we make influence everything we build. The presentation focused on three principles that form an important part of the business philosophy of DevGameBlogPromo.

Our Core Principles

Three principles. One foundation.

The values that guide how we approach business, relationships and long-term growth.

01

Integrity

Doing what is right, even when nobody is watching. Building trust through honest decisions, accountability and consistency between words and actions.

02

Authenticity

Staying true to our identity, beliefs and purpose. Genuine relationships are built through transparency, honesty and being real.

03

Discipline

Showing up consistently and taking responsibility for progress. Meaningful results are built through repeated effort, commitment and habits.

Audience & Engagement

Sharing ideas with the next generation.

The presentation was delivered in front of an audience of more than 400 students. Opportunities like these allow DevGameBlogPromo to share perspectives, encourage critical thinking and connect with students beyond traditional learning environments.
