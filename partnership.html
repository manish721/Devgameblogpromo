Official DGBP Partnership Policy

Build together.
Be clear about the deal.

DevGameBlogPromo welcomes meaningful partnerships with developers, creators, companies, educational organizations, communities and other organizations.

Submit a Proposal Read the Policy

Important Payment Notice

DevGameBlogPromo does not automatically pay for partnership, promotion, sponsorship, content, exposure, referrals, introductions, services or other collaboration proposals. If you expect payment from DGBP, you must clearly identify your proposal as a Paid Collaboration / Paid Partnership and specify the proposed amount, deliverables and terms.

01 — Partnership Policy

How partnerships work

We want partnership discussions to be transparent from the beginning. Neither side should make assumptions about money, deliverables or responsibilities.

🤝

Mutual Partnerships

DGBP may collaborate through mutually agreed value exchange, including community access, technology, education, content, visibility, resources or expertise.

₹

No Automatic Payment

Submitting a partnership proposal does not create a payment obligation. DGBP does not assume that a proposal is paid unless payment has been explicitly agreed.

✓

Paid Partnerships

If your proposal requires DGBP to pay you, clearly identify it as a Paid Collaboration / Paid Partnership and provide the proposed price, scope, deliverables and timeline.

📋

Written Agreement

Any payment, sponsorship, commercial service or financial commitment must be agreed by DGBP before the relevant work begins.

🔍

Proposal Review

Every proposal may be reviewed based on relevance, value, credibility, audience fit, resources, timing and DGBP's current priorities.

✕

No Guaranteed Approval

Sending a proposal does not guarantee acceptance, publication, promotion, payment, partnership status or future collaboration.

02 — Payment Rules

Be explicit about money.

Payment expectations must be clear before a commercial collaboration begins.

Standard Partnership

No Payment Requested

This is the standard partnership model. Both parties contribute agreed value without DGBP automatically paying the partner.

  • No automatic payment from DGBP
  • Value exchange must be mutually agreed
  • Deliverables should be clearly defined
  • Approval is required before representing the partnership
Commercial Partnership

Paid Collaboration

If you want DGBP to pay you, this must be proposed and approved as a Paid Collaboration / Paid Partnership.

  • State the exact proposed payment
  • Explain what DGBP receives
  • Define deliverables and timeline
  • Payment terms must be agreed in writing
  • No payment should be assumed before approval
03 — Process

From proposal to partnership

1

Prepare your proposal

Collect all required information listed in the proposal section below.

2

Send it by email

Partnership proposals are accepted through email only. Use the email button below to open a prepared proposal.

3

DGBP reviews the proposal

DGBP may evaluate relevance, feasibility, value, credibility, timing and current business priorities.

4

Discuss the terms

If there is mutual interest, both parties can discuss scope, responsibilities, deliverables, timeline and payment terms.

5

Agreement before execution

Commercial work or payment commitments should not begin until the applicable terms have been explicitly accepted by DGBP.

04 — Proposal

Submit a partnership proposal

DGBP accepts partnership proposals by email only. Please include all of the following information in your email.

1

Name / Organization

Full name, organization/company name, website and relevant social media or professional profiles.

2

Contact Information

Email, phone/WhatsApp if applicable, city and country.

3

Partnership Type

Clearly state whether the proposal is Strategic, Technology, Education, Community, Content / Media, Marketing / Promotion, Paid Collaboration / Paid Partnership, or another type.

4

Purpose of Partnership

Explain why you want to partner with DevGameBlogPromo and exactly what you are proposing.

5

What You Provide

Explain everything you or your organization will contribute, including content, promotion, technology, services, audience/community access, education, events, marketing or other resources.

6

What You Expect From DGBP

Clearly state everything you expect DevGameBlogPromo to provide or do.

7

Deliverables

List the exact deliverables expected from you and from DGBP.

8

Timeline

Include proposed start date, end date, duration, deadlines and important milestones.

9

Payment

State clearly whether payment from DGBP is requested. If yes, provide the exact amount, currency, payment schedule, deliverables and what DGBP receives in return. If no payment is requested, clearly state that.

10

Other Terms

Mention any additional conditions, exclusivity, usage rights, approvals, resources, responsibilities or other requirements.

11

Previous Work / Proof

Include portfolio links, previous partnerships, campaigns, projects, case studies or other relevant proof.

12

Final Confirmation

Confirm that you understand that submitting a proposal does not guarantee approval, partnership status, publication, promotion or payment.

Payment Policy — Please Read

DevGameBlogPromo does not automatically pay for partnerships, promotions, sponsorships, content, exposure, referrals, introductions, services or other collaboration proposals.

If you require DGBP to pay you, you must clearly identify your proposal as a Paid Collaboration / Paid Partnership and provide the proposed amount, deliverables, commercial terms and what DGBP receives in return.

No payment should be assumed unless DGBP explicitly reviews and agrees to the proposed paid partnership terms.
