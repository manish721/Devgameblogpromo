How it works

From zero to shipped, in four steps.

No vague "modules" — this is the actual path every student walks.

Step 1

Apply & pick a track

Fill the enrollment form, choose Frontend, AI/ML, or Cloud based on where you want to be in 6 months.

Step 2

Onboard on Discord

You get access to the cohort channel, session schedule, and the first assignment within 24 hours.

Step 3

Build in public

Live sessions plus self-paced project work. Every project is reviewed directly, not auto-graded.

Step 4

Ship & get certified

You leave with 3-4 deployed projects, a completion certificate, and direct feedback on your portfolio.

Questions

Before you enroll.

What courses does DGBPLabZ offer?
Frontend Web Development (₹499), AI & Machine Learning (₹799), and Cloud & DevOps (₹999) — all project-based with live mentorship.
Is this suitable for complete beginners?
Yes — the Frontend track assumes zero prior experience. You build something real from week one.
Are sessions live or recorded?
Live via Discord, recorded for reference. Project work is self-paced within the cohort window.
How do I enroll?
Fill in the enrollment form or join the Discord — you'll hear back within 24 hours.
Who actually reviews my projects?
Manish reviews project submissions directly. There's no anonymous grading queue — feedback comes from the person who taught the material.
What if I want a refund?
Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis. Please contact us directly for more information.
