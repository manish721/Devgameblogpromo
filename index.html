Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining DGBPLabZ. Still have questions? See our full FAQ page or email us directly.

What courses does DGBPLabZ offer? ▾

DGBPLabZ offers three structured programs: Frontend Web Development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git) starting at ₹499; AI & Machine Learning with Python including prompt engineering, starting at ₹799; and Cloud Architecture & DevOps (AWS, Azure, CI/CD) starting at ₹999. All courses are project-based with live mentorship sessions.

Is DGBPLabZ suitable for complete beginners? ▾

Yes. The Frontend Development course is designed for absolute beginners — no prior coding experience required. The structured, project-first curriculum means you build something real from week one, supported by full mentor guidance throughout the program.

How much do DGBPLabZ courses cost, and what's included? ▾

Courses start at ₹499 for Frontend Development, ₹799 for AI/ML, and ₹999 for Cloud & DevOps. Each course includes live mentorship sessions, project reviews, Discord community access, a portfolio of real projects, and a completion certificate from DevGameBlogPromo — all at a one-time price.

Are sessions live or recorded? Can I learn at my own pace? ▾

DGBPLabZ combines live mentorship sessions with flexible project work. Core sessions are held live via Discord and recorded for reference. Project work is self-paced within cohort timelines, giving you the structure of a live cohort with flexibility for real-life schedules.

Do I receive a certificate after completing a course? ▾

Yes. Students who complete their course and project requirements receive a completion certificate issued by DevGameBlogPromo, the operating organisation behind DGBPLabZ. Certificates document your skills and project portfolio, and can be verified by employers upon request.

Is DGBPLabZ based in Hyderabad? Can I join from anywhere in India? ▾

DGBPLabZ is operated by DevGameBlogPromo, headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. All courses are available fully online, so students from anywhere in India — or internationally — can enroll and participate in live sessions via Discord.

How is DGBPLabZ different from other coding bootcamps in India? ▾

Unlike most bootcamps that rush through content and focus on certificates, DGBPLabZ prioritises project delivery and systems thinking. You graduate with a live, demonstrable portfolio rather than just course completion data. Mentorship is direct — you learn from Kakarla Manish Reddy and senior mentors who are active practitioners, not hired instructors reading slides. Prices start at ₹499, making high-quality mentorship genuinely accessible.

How do I apply or enroll? ▾

You can apply by filling in the enrollment form here or by joining our Discord community. Our team will respond within 24 hours to guide you through course selection, payment, and your first session.
