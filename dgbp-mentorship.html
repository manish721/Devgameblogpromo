Eligibility & Access Rules

Mentorship Fee: ₹0 (Completely Free)

  • 01Students enrolled in DGBP courses can access personal mentorship completely free of charge.
  • 02Students who have not enrolled in any DGBP course can also access personal mentorship completely free of charge.
  • 03All students must follow the official DGBP mentorship guidelines and code of conduct.
  • 04One-on-one mentorship is provided on selected dates only and is subject to mentor availability.
  • 05There are no fixed session limits. Students may request mentorship whenever required, subject to available dates and scheduling.
  • 06One-on-one sessions must be scheduled and confirmed in advance.
  • 07DGBP reserves the right to modify mentorship schedules based on mentor availability and programme requirements.

How it works

1. Click Book a session

Mentorship days repeat on the same dates every month (15th, 17th, 19th, 21st). Open dates in the next 30 days are highlighted.

2. Fill the email

Select a 20-minute time slot (IST) and tell us what you want to work on.

3. Get confirmed

We confirm your session in advance by email or WhatsApp.

4. Learn & build

Join your one-on-one session. Request another whenever you need.
