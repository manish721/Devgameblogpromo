01 — WHO WE ARE

More than a learning platform.
A place to move ideas forward.

DevGameBlogPromo (DGBP) is a founder-led initiative working across education, technology, research and innovation. Our goal is to make learning more practical and help people move from consuming information to understanding, creating and applying it.

We believe access to videos and documents is only a starting point. People also need direction, thoughtful guidance, opportunities to practise, and the confidence to ask better questions. We are building around that complete journey.

DGBP is the organisation and central identity. Its initiatives and projects contribute to a wider ecosystem, each with its own focus and development stage.
